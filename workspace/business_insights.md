## What Matters Most
The customer_state matters most for predicting late delivery cases because regional logistics networks and local carrier coverage vary significantly, meaning late delivery depends heavily on the state where the order is delivered. The order_estimated_delivery_date is the second most relevant factor for cases of late delivery, as orders with shorter deadlines create logistical pressure that increases the risk of late delivery. The purchase_to_estimated_days is the third most relevant factor, since the number of days between purchasing an order and the expected delivery date influences the complexity of fulfillment, where longer transit windows can create scheduling bottlenecks that lead to late delivery. The customer_zip_code_prefix is the fourth most relevant factor for late delivery instances, because specific local routing areas have unique delivery challenges and carrier reliability differences that affect whether late delivery occurs for those orders.
